How your data becomes a dashboard
No engineering background required — here's exactly what happens between "you connect a tool" and "you have a dashboard," in plain language.

You shouldn't have to trust a black box with your sales and customer data. So instead of asking you to take our word for it, here's the actual path your data takes — from the systems you already use, to the dashboard you open every morning.

1

You connect your tools

You link the systems you already run — your point of sale, your online store, your accounting software, your CRM — through each tool's own secure login screen. The same kind of "Connect with Google" flow you already trust elsewhere. We never see or store your passwords, and we never touch your database directly — only what you've explicitly approved.

→
2

We sync it automatically

Every day, a scheduled job checks each connected tool for anything new or changed, pulls it in, and reshapes it into one clean, consistent format. No manual exports, no spreadsheets emailed back and forth.

→
3

We do the analysis for you

Trends, comparisons, forecasts, and anomaly checks are computed ahead of time and stored — before you ever open the dashboard. That's the difference between a dashboard that thinks in front of you (slow, and someone has to know what to ask for) and one that already knows the answer.

→
4

Your data is walled off — twice

Every business's data lives in its own secure space, enforced at the database level, not only in our application's code. Even in the event of a bug in our app, the database itself refuses to let one client see another client's numbers. That two-layer approach is called defense in depth — the same principle banks use.

→
5

You just open a link

No software to install, no new logins to manage, works on any device. If you can open a bookmark, you can use your dashboard.

Common questions

What business owners actually ask us

Do you ever see or store my passwords?

No. Every connection uses that tool's own official login and permission screen (OAuth) — the same standard behind "Sign in with Google" or "Connect to Slack." We receive a limited, revocable access token, never your credentials, and you can revoke it at any time from the source tool itself.

Can other clients see my data?

No. Isolation is enforced at the database level (Postgres row-level security), not just in application code. A bug in our app logic still can't leak data across clients, because the database itself rejects the query — that's the "walled off twice" step above.

What if I switch point-of-sale or accounting systems?

Tell us and we reconnect the new source. Your historical data and dashboards stay intact — we map the new system's data into the same normalized schema the old one used.

How often does my data update?

Daily by default, on a schedule that runs overnight so your numbers are ready when you sit down in the morning. If your business needs same-day or hourly numbers, that's available too — it's a configuration change, not a rebuild.

Do I need any technical skills to use this?

No. You get a link. If you can open a website, you can use your dashboard — no installs, no logins beyond your own, no training session required.
